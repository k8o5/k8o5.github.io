How it works

Deploy a token on a live bonding curve — plus, optionally, a real SPL mint. The one thing that can't change: 1% of every trade buys SOL for your holders.

01 · Deploy

One form, optional site

Set the name, ticker, supply and your first buy — and optionally insert or upload one HTML file that becomes the token's own page. The token opens on a bonding curve instantly.

02 · Trade

Live depth, real slippage

Buy and sell against the curve with a visible depth chart, price impact and a 1% fee. Every trade is recorded into real candles — no simulated numbers anywhere.

03 · Earn

Holders get paid in SOL

70% of every fee buys SOL for holders, proportional to their share — no swap, it arrives in SOL already. Plus: mint the real SPL token and open a real Raydium pool.
