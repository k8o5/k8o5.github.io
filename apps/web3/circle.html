Circle is a non-custodial rotating savings circle: a group chips the same amount every week into a pot, and each round one person takes it all. No bank, no middleman, no "trust me" — the smart contract always pays the pot, and you can only ever be the pot loser, never the bag. Compiled on-chain from a single HTML file.

How a circle works

① Everyone chips the same weekly amount into the contract.
② At the end of each cycle the contract pays the entire pot to the next member in line — automatically, provably.
③ It keeps rotating until everyone has been paid once.
④ Behind on chips? Your late peer keeps their collateral. Even if everyone else flakes, the pot is always real SOL paying out on schedule.
The rules: scrubbed to the order of member slots, fully deterministic.

Circle is an experiment in trustless community money.
The entire app is this one circle.html file: no backend, no account database, no custody.
The Solana program is minted onto the chain at deploy time from WASM compiled in your browser — so the code that's running is the code you're reading.
Circle adapts the tanda / cundina / sou-sou / chama / gam'eya — a financial tradition older than banking — to a blockchain where nobody holds the pot but everyone can watch it.
