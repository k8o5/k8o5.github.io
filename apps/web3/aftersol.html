❓ Hard questions, honest answers

What if I just forget to check in?

Grace escalation: reminder → alert → final warning across the last 20% of your window (email/push via optional notifier, plus on-chain events any watcher can surface). 90-day default means you'd have to ignore 3 weeks of warnings. And even then — cancel/reclaim is one tx if you're alive.

What if an heir loses their wallet?

Owner can update heirs anytime while alive. Pro tip: register heirs as fresh addresses whose seed is stored with the family lawyer — not their hot trading wallet.

Can Aftersol steal my funds?

No. Demo holds nothing (local only). Mainnet design is a program-owned PDA: the program's rules are the only way funds move, and claims can only pay registered heirs. No admin key, no custodian, no "support" that can touch it.

Why Solana and not Ethereum?

Heartbeat economics. A $0.001 check-in you can do monthly for a decade costs cents. On L1 Ethereum that's a car payment. Solana's speed also enables instant permissionless claims by any watcher.
