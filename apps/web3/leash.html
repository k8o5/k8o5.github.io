Why this doesn't exist yet

Agents can think.
Nobody lets them spend — safely.

Today's options are all-or-nothing: give an agent your seed phrase (insane) or approve every payment by hand (then it isn't autonomous). LEASH is the middle layer the next cycle needs.

🔐

Bounded delegation

The vault holds only what you fund it with. The agent key can move funds only within policy: per-tx cap, rolling 24h budget, expiry. It can never touch your main wallet — the leash is mathematical, not a pinky promise.

📜

On-chain audit trail

Every payment carries a Memo instruction: merchant, amount, policy id, timestamp. Your agent's entire financial life becomes a public, immutable ledger — auditable by you, your CFO, or a regulator.

🩸

Kill switch

Agent misbehaving? One click sweeps every lamport back to your wallet and anchors a revocation notice on-chain. The leash key becomes worthless instantly. This is the feature x402-style agent payments are missing today.

From prototype to protocol

What this becomes

This page is a fully working client-side proof of the trust model — bounded keys, memo-anchored audit, kill switch — executed with real mainnet transactions. The production version moves enforcement into a program:

Leash program (Anchor)
// policy enforced on-chain, not in the browser
pub struct Leash {
    owner:      Pubkey,   // you — can always revoke
    agent:      Pubkey,   // bounded key
    per_tx_cap: u64,
    daily_cap:  u64,
    spent_24h:  u64,
    allowlist:  Vec<Pubkey>,
    expires_at: i64,
    revoked:    bool,
}
// agent calls:  leashed_pay(amount, merchant)
// owner calls:  revoke_and_sweep()
// the PDA vault rejects anything outside policy —
// even if the agent key is compromised.
Why it wins
1

x402 & agent payments are exploding — with zero guardrails

HTTP-native agent payments let bots pay APIs per-request. Today the only "control" is hoping your prompt behaves. LEASH is the control plane.

2

Every company will have a fleet of spending agents

Procurement bots, data buyers, infra autoscalers. They need corporate cards with limits — on-chain. That's a leash: vault + policy + audit.

3

Network-native reputation falls out for free

An agent that pays thousands of invoices within policy, zero revocations, builds a verifiable credit history. Lenders and counterparties can trust agents they've never met.

4

Composability

Policies as NFTs, programmable allowlists, DAO-governed leashes, insurance on leash-bonded agents. The primitive generalizes.
