How it works

01 · One tx

All or nothing

The same transaction creates the mint account (82 B), opens your token account (165 B), initializes the mint — 8 decimals, no freeze authority — and mints the full supply to your wallet. If any step fails, none of it happens.

02 · Yours

Plain, no program in the middle

Mint authority is your wallet; there is no launchpad account owning the curve. Name, image and links go to IPFS and are referenced by the mint, so trackers can show it.

03 · Tradeable

When you add liquidity

A plain token has no pool yet. Add one on Raydium or Meteora whenever you want it tradeable — or just transfer the tokens. Nothing to graduate, nothing to pay up front.
