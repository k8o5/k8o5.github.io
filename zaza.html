How it works

Create a local simulation, or connect your wallet to additionally mint a real SPL token.

01 · Deploy

One form, optional site

Set the name, ticker, supply and your first buy — and optionally insert or upload one HTML file that becomes the token's own page. The token opens on a bonding curve instantly.

02 · Trade

Explore simulated trades

Practice buys and sells with paper balances. Charts, slippage and fees model a local curve; no assets move on-chain.

03 · Earn

Understand the economics

Explore simulated fee distributions. Actual trading and payouts require a separate deployed program and funded liquidity pool.
