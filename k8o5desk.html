How it works

A launch is an ordinary coin on a live bonding curve — plus, optionally, a real SPL token. The one thing that can't change: 1% of every trade buys the pair asset for your holders.

01 · Launch

Pick a real asset, take a first buy

Choose what holders get paid in — a tokenized stock (AAPLx, NVDAx, TSLAx…), a crypto (SOL, BTC, ETH…), or USDC — and how much SOL your first buy is. The coin opens on a bonding curve instantly.

02 · Trade

Live depth, real slippage

Buy and sell against the curve with a visible depth chart, price impact and a 1% fee. Every trade is recorded into real candles — no simulated numbers anywhere.

03 · Earn

Holders get paid in the pair asset

70% of every fee buys the coin's paired asset for holders, proportional to their share. High-value pairs like BTC pay out in sats — 1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats — so nothing rounds to zero. Plus: mint the real SPL token and open a real Raydium pool.
